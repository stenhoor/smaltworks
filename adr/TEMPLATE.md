# ADR NNNN: Title in sentence case

- **Status:** proposed | accepted (date, by whom) | deferred | superseded by ADR NNNN | done
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Tier:** 1 (project-wide) | 2 (repository-internal)
- **Repositories:** the repositories this decision binds
- **Builds on:** earlier ADRs or decisions, linked

## Context

What problem, and what forces bear on it. Link the goal or the standards it serves.

## Decision

What was decided, in numbered points where there are several. Record the owner's decisions in their words where they
were given.

## Consequences

What follows: the work in each repository, what gets easier or harder, what is ruled out.

## Considered

The alternatives that were weighed, and why they weren't chosen.
